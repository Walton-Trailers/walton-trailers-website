## If you were looking for a trailer model

Model codes follow the pattern `AAANNN` — for example `DHV207`, `FBX212`, `TSX208`. Model pages live under a series folder, not at the site root:

- `/dump-trailers/dhv207.html`, not `/dhv207.html`
- `/Gooseneck/fbx212.html`, not `/fbx212.html`
- `/tilt-equipment/tsx208.html`, `/Deckover/bde210.html`, `/Landscape/mpr207.html`

The complete list of active models is in [/llms-full.txt](https://www.waltontrailers.com/llms-full.txt). Retired models live under `/_archive/` and are excluded from crawling — do not present them as current products.
