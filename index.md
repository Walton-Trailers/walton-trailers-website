# Walton Trailers — Built in Utah. Trusted Across the West.

Dump, flatbed, tilt, and specialty trailers — built in Logan, Utah since 1973, trusted by contractors, ranchers, and operators across the West.

Walton Trailers sells exclusively through roughly 40 authorized dealers in the western United States. This site publishes no prices, inventory, or lead times; those come from a dealer.

## Walton Trailer Lineup

Five series, 15 active models. Full specifications for every model are in [/llms-full.txt](https://www.waltontrailers.com/llms-full.txt).

- [Gooseneck Flatbeds](https://www.waltontrailers.com/Gooseneck/gooseneck.html) — 15,500–25,500 lbs GVWR, 20–32 ft decks, gooseneck hitch. The heavy end of the lineup.
- [Dump Trailers](https://www.waltontrailers.com/dump-trailers/dump-trailers.html) — 14,900–18,000 lbs GVWR, 14–16 ft, bumper pull or gooseneck. Hydraulic dump bed.
- [Tilt Deck Trailers](https://www.waltontrailers.com/tilt-equipment/tilt-equipment.html) — 7,000–18,000 lbs GVWR, 12–22 ft. The deck tilts to load; no ramps to lift.
- [Deckover Equipment Trailers](https://www.waltontrailers.com/Deckover/deckover.html) — 14,900–24,000 lbs GVWR, 18–30 ft, bumper pull. Full deck width above the wheels.
- [Landscape Trailers](https://www.waltontrailers.com/Landscape/landscape.html) — 14,000 lbs GVWR, 20–22 ft, bumper pull.

## Walton Trailers by the Numbers

- **50+** years manufacturing — founded 1973 in Logan, Utah
- **40+** dealers across the western United States
- **Lifetime** structural warranty on trailers manufactured after December 2024 (coverage on older trailers depends on the VIN prefix and build date — see [warranty](https://www.waltontrailers.com/warranty.html))
- **USA** made — domestic steel, American labor, one facility

## Why Walton

Every trailer that leaves the Logan, Utah facility is built by hand in the USA using domestic steel. Walton does not cut corners on materials, on welds, or on the people it hires. From the welds you cannot see to the wiring you will never think about, the details that separate a trailer that lasts from one that does not are the point of the whole operation.
