Built for the Long Haul

Heavy Work Demands a Heavy-Duty Trailer

Walton Gooseneck Flatbeds are built from high-strength steel with fully welded frames — not bolted. Every weld is inspected before it leaves our Logan, Utah facility.

Whether you're hauling equipment across a job site or running cross-state, these trailers are engineered to handle the load and keep working long after lesser trailers give out.

Overview Photo — Trailer loaded with equipment

Built Different

Fully Welded Frame

No bolts, no flex points. The entire frame is welded and stress-tested before leaving the factory floor.

Lifetime Structural Warranty

We stand behind every trailer we build. The structural warranty is lifetime — no fine print, no exceptions.

Made in Logan, Utah

Every trailer is manufactured start-to-finish in our Logan, Utah facility. American steel, American labor.

Multiple Deck Lengths

Choose from 20' to 40' deck lengths. We build to your application, not the other way around.

Heavy-Duty Axles

Industry-leading axle ratings with sealed bearings and integrated brake systems built for the long haul.

Corrosion Protection

Primer, intermediate, and finish coats applied in sequence. Built to resist rust for the life of the trailer.
