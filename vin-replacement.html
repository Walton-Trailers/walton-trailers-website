VIN Replacement

Request a replacement VIN sticker for your Walton trailer.

🛑 STOP: Read Before Submitting Your Request

To prevent vehicle theft and comply with legal requirements, all 4 items below are required. Incomplete submissions will be automatically rejected.

  1. Proof of Ownership (Clear copy of Title, Active Registration, or MSO)
  2. Government-Issued Photo ID (Must match the name on the ownership document)
  3. Photo of Welded VIN Digits (Location: The last 3 digits of the VIN are welded onto the inner side of the metal frame on the passenger side)
  4. Photo of the Full Trailer (Side view showing the entire trailer)
VIN Replacement Request
All fields are required except Trailer Model. Your request cannot be submitted without the four attachments.
