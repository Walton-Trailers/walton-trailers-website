16K Gravity Tilt — Oil Bath Axles, Heavier Capacity

The TSX208 steps up to 17,200–18,000 lbs GVWR with Dexter Torflex Oil Bath axles and a stiffer 3" channel cross member spacing. The beveled knife edge keeps loading clean and tool-free. Available in bumper pull (up to 24') and gooseneck (up to 24') configurations.

The frame steps up to 2" × 6" tube on the gooseneck version for the added tongue load. D-rings, rubrail, toolbox, and gravity cushion cylinder all remain standard.
